## What Chippy is
A single-file, browser-native, generative chiptune dance-music instrument + light show. Generation,
not playback. Companion to OMS Dojo (the performance instrument) and OMS Sozo (the AMU analysis app).
"Chippy is a techno rave chiptune love song generator."
